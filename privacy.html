Privacy Policy

దుఆ & జిక్ర్ (Dua & Zikr) – Android app by TeluguIslam.net · Effective 11 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your bookmarks, settings and reading history stay on your phone.
  • The app uses the internet only to play audio and to download corrected book texts.
  • If you choose to report a mistake, only what you type in that form is sent to us.

Data stored on your phone

Bookmarks, the last place you read, recently read duas, recent searches, your settings (text size, theme, …), downloaded audio, and error reports waiting to be sent while offline. This data never leaves your phone, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet use

WhatWhereSent
Book updates (checked when the app starts)teluguislamnet.github.io (GitHub Pages)A normal web request; no personal data

Like any website, these servers receive your device's IP address with each request. We do not use it to identify you.

Error reports

From a dua's ⋮ menu you can report a mistake. The report contains: which book, dua and chapter; which part (Arabic, transliteration, …); the words you selected or typed, your correction and description; the app and content version; and a link to the dua on telugudua.net. Optionally, a WhatsApp number, only if you type one, so we can reply. Reports are used only to correct the books and to answer you. They are not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. To have a report deleted, write to us (below).

Delete your data

The app has no account, so there is no account to delete. Everything else is on your phone or in an error report you chose to send.

  1. Data on your phone (bookmarks, reading history, recent searches, settings, downloaded audio, unsent reports): Android Settings → Apps → దుఆ & జిక్ర్ → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Downloaded audio alone: in the app, Settings → ఆఫ్‌లైన్ ఆడియో → ఫోన్ నుండి తొలగించు.
  2. Error reports you sent (including any WhatsApp number you typed): email teluguislam.net@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my report” and the WhatsApp number you used, or the dua and the day you sent it. We delete the matching reports, with everything in them, within 30 days and reply to confirm. We keep nothing else about you.

Sharing

When you share a dua (text or image), Android's share menu sends it to the app you choose. The app does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
